# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful,
assume good intent while evaluating technical claims against evidence, accept constructive
feedback, and protect the privacy of conversation archives used during development.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publishing another person's private information, or sharing raw chat evidence without
permission. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to keep the community safe.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in the GitHub
repository. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and respond proportionately.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced by the
full current Contributor Covenant before a major public release.
